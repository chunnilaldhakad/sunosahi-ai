**Project title:** SunoSahi AI — Two-Party Voice Agreement Verification

**One-line summary:** Consent-first, multilingual voice-assisted verification that catches contradictory daily wage rates and workdays before workers and contractors record an apparent agreement.

**Problem solved:** Informal wage agreements often rely on oral numbers. Hindi/Hinglish speech, background noise and changing terms can cause confusion: “15 versus 50 days” or “₹650 versus ₹560/day”.
